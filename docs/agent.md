# FarmAI Agent Capabilities

FarmAI utilizes several Artificial Intelligence models to provide an intelligent agent ecosystem for rural farmers. The agent primarily interacts with users through the FarmAI Mobile Application in the Bengali language.

## AI Models
- **CNN Image Classification Model**: Analyzes images uploaded by farmers to detect wounds, skin problems, or swelling.
- **Whisper Speech-to-Text**: Processes Bengali voice input, allowing illiterate or less tech-savvy farmers to communicate easily.
- **Qwen 3.6 27B (LLM)**: Powers the Bangla AI Voice Chat and Symptom Checker, providing fast and highly accurate natural language responses for feeding advice, disease care, and general farming support.

## Core AI Features

### 1. AI Symptoms & Image Check
Farmers can upload images of their livestock's visible symptoms. The AI Agent:
- Suggests possible causes of the symptoms.
- Determines risk levels: **Normal**, **Vet Soon**, or **Emergency**.
- Provides step-by-step recommendations and safe supportive care practices.
- Advises on immediate veterinary support for serious cases.

### 2. Bangla AI Voice Chat
An interactive voice assistant that communicates with farmers in Bengali, providing:
- Feeding advice
- Animal care guidance
- Prevention tips
- Vaccination information
- General farming support

### 3. Smart Hardware Integration (IoT)
The AI agent processes data from the FarmAI Smart Collar:
- **Activity Monitoring**: The agent analyzes motion sensor data to detect abnormal inactivity, automatically generating health risk alerts and recommending veterinary consultation if necessary.
- **Vaccine/Deworming Schedules**: Triggers follow-up reminders, mobile notifications, and activates LED lights on the collar when dates arrive.

### 4. Automated Operations
